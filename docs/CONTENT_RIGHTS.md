# Content Rights Checklist

Before publishing a programme, channel, film, podcast, music track, artwork, or live stream:

- Identify the rights owner and authorised representative.
- Obtain written permission or a signed distribution agreement.
- Confirm that the grant explicitly covers online streaming and the intended platforms.
- Record territory, licence term, permitted uses, catch-up/clips, artwork, subtitles, and advertising permissions.
- Store the agreement reference, start date, expiry date, and internal reviewer.
- Confirm the stream URL is authorised for this use and does not violate provider terms.
- Prevent publication until the required rights checks are complete.
- Review upcoming expiries and unpublish content when rights expire or are withdrawn.
- Keep an audit trail of approvals and changes.

Free-to-air availability does not automatically permit online retransmission.
